A hyper-modern Android calculator and homage to the HP-12C. Real RPN, the real 12c keyboard key for key, 34 significant digits in every register, and an 80s green-screen display. It shows up in your app drawer as Calculator (bf-12c).

What's on the keys

The 12c, all of it

Loans, mortgages, NPV, IRR, amortization, bonds, depreciation, dates, statistics and keystroke programs, the way the 12c does them. Landscape is the full keyboard; portrait folds it in half, financial keys on top and the number pad where your thumbs are.

PREFIX
ENTERLSTx

Real RPN

A four-level stack (X Y Z T), LSTx, and stack lift that behaves like the real thing. Continuous memory: the stack, the registers and your program survive restarts.

RND
PMTCFj

Time value of money

n i PV PMT FV: solve for any one of them. BEGIN / END, 12× and 12÷, amortization schedules and simple interest.

IRR
FVNj

Cash flows, bonds, dates

NPV and IRR over up to 20 cash-flow groups, bond price and yield, SL / SOYD / DB depreciation, and dates and the days between them.

ALL
EEXΔDYS

34 significant digits

Every register is a 34-digit decimal, not a floating-point double. f EEX shows all of them; when the display is rounded, portrait shows the full value beneath it.

P/R
R/SPSE

Keystroke programs

99 merged lines with GTO, x≤y, x=0, pause and single-step both ways. The listing shows the 12c keycodes and a readable mnemonic.

Σ+Σ−

Math and statistics

%, Δ%, %T, mean, standard deviation, weighted mean, linear estimate and correlation, 20 storage registers with STO arithmetic, yˣ, eˣ, LN, n!.

−←

On the glass

Swipe the display to backspace, long-press to copy X, double-tap to paste. Paste reads numbers the way people copy them: $1,000, (250.00), 6.02e23.

MENU

The MENU key

Where the 12c's ON key sits: four phosphors, haptics, a built-in manual and FAQ, the tips, and a way to report a bug. It never nags you to rate or share.

⌨TalkBack

Every screen, every input

Phones, tablets, foldables and Chromebooks, split screen or desktop window. Touch, mouse, a hardware keyboard, or TalkBack, which reads every key in words.

Screens

Folded, programmed, lit

The layout follows the window, not the device. Portrait folds the keyboard; tablets and unfolded foldables print the legends, registers and menu bigger.

MENU › Theme

Four phosphors

Pick the glass. The keyboard keeps the 12c's gold and blue whichever you choose.

No nonsense

Nothing leaves the phone

bf-12c asks for no permissions at all, not even the network. No accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and nothing is backed up off the device. Rating and sharing are menu items, there when you want them, never a prompt.

Get it

Now on Google Play

bf-12c is free on Google Play: no ads, no in-app purchases, no permissions. It runs on phones, tablets, foldables and Chromebooks with Android 13 or newer, and Play keeps it up to date.

FEEDBACKGitHub issue

  1. Install from Google Play

    Open the bf-12c listing and tap Install. It shows up in your app drawer as Calculator (bf-12c).

  2. Or sideload the APK

    Prefer no store? Grab bf-12c.apk from the latest release. Every push to main publishes one date-labeled release and replaces the last.

  3. Verify

    Check the APK against the attached bf-12c.apk.sha256. The release also carries a build-provenance attestation.
