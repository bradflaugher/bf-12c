The ON key now reads MENU. On the real 12c it switches the calculator on and off; here it has always opened the system menu (phosphors, haptics, the manual and FAQ, tips, share, rate and feedback), and now it says so. It sits in the same place with the same keycode, so programs and listings are unchanged. TalkBack still calls it "menu", and F1 or the Menu key still opens it from a keyboard.
