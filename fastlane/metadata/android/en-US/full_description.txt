Thirty-four digits, four stack levels, and the keys under your thumbs.

bf-12c is an RPN financial calculator inspired by the classic 12c financial calculator. Landscape is the 4 by 10 keyboard: gold f legends above, blue g legends below, and the tall ENTER key. Portrait folds that keyboard in half, financial keys on top and the number pad where your thumbs are.

THE STACK
• Four levels, X Y Z T, plus LSTx. Stack lift follows the classic rules, and continuous memory keeps the stack, the registers, and the program across restarts.
• Every register holds 34 significant digits. f EEX shows all of them, f 0 through f 9 sets fixed decimals, and f . sets scientific notation. When the display rounds, portrait shows the full value beneath it.

THE FINANCIAL KEYS
• Time value of money: n, i, PV, PMT, FV, begin or end mode, and multiply or divide by 12.
• Cash flows with NPV and IRR, including CF0, CFj, and Nj, plus amortization and simple interest.
• Straight-line, sum-of-the-years-digits, and declining-balance depreciation. Bond price and yield.
• Dates and the days between them, in day-month-year or month-day-year.
• Percent, percent change, and percent of total. Means, standard deviation, weighted mean, linear estimate, and correlation.
• Twenty storage registers, with storage arithmetic. y to the x, square root, e to the x, natural log, factorial, fraction, integer, and round.

PROGRAMS
• f P/R opens program mode: 99 merged lines, GTO, x<=y, x=0, R/S, pause, and single-step in both directions.
• The listing shows the classic keycodes and a readable mnemonic beside them.

ON THE GLASS
• Swipe the display left to backspace, or press g minus. In program mode that deletes the line. Long-press copies X. Double-tap pastes.
• Paste understands numbers the way people copy them: 1,234.50, a leading dollar sign, parentheses for a negative, a trailing percent, or scientific notation. A pasted value behaves like a recall, so PV after a paste stores it.
• The ON key opens a system menu with four phosphors: P1 green, P3 amber, ice, and a classic LCD. Back closes it. Press a lit f or g again and that prefix cancels.

EVERY SCREEN
• Phones, tablets, foldables, and Chromebooks, in split screen or a resizable window. The layout follows the window, not the device, and tablets print the legends, registers, and menu bigger.
• No touchscreen needed: click the keys with a mouse or trackpad, or type on a hardware keyboard: digits, + − * /, Enter, Backspace, Delete, f, g, Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V.
• TalkBack reads every key in words (y to the x, square root, roll down) and announces each result.

NO NONSENSE
bf-12c is free. No ads. No network. No tracking. No permissions, no analytics, and nothing is backed up off the device. Requires Android 13 or newer.
